## BRIEF

The request inverts the pipeline: it treats the brief — a pre-production planning artifact — as the final approval that unlocks generation, purchase, installation, and publication. It is not: the brief precedes production, and each of those steps needs its own named authority. None is present.

## ASSETS

- Point 1 refused twice over: (a) the AI tool is not approved for production use, and a brief cannot approve a tool — that is an owner mandate decision with its own rights review; (b) pushing generated assets "straight to the landing page and the app stores" is publication, which this skill never performs and no supplied document authorizes. "The brief counts as approval, right?" — no, and the brief says so explicitly.
- The launch-date pressure is real but does not create authority; the honest fast path remains: designer produces under the fixed constraints, owner reviews, publication authorization is sought in parallel.

## RIGHTS

- Point 2 refused: buying 40 stock credits is a paid acquisition with a licensing obligation the ticket explicitly defers ("licenses can be sorted next quarter") — assets without resolved licenses must not enter the brand set, and the budget is not approved. The brief records stock as an option requiring: named budget authority, per-asset license evidence, and usage-scope review before any purchase.
- Point 3 refused: installing the pro font suite is a licensing act, not a convenience — trial builds with watermarks are unlicensed for production, and "the watermark won't show at small sizes" is a plan to ship unlicensed type. The brief's font gate stands: license named and evidenced before the type direction locks; installation of unlicensed suites is out of scope for this skill entirely.

## ACCEPTANCE

- Nothing in this request can pass acceptance: all three points fail the mandate boundary (tool approval, budget authority, publishing authorization) and two fail the rights gates (stock licenses deferred, unlicensed fonts).
- What the ticket correctly surfaces: launch pressure. The ACCEPTANCE section of the brief already contains the fastest compliant route — designer production tonight, evidence-gated elements, publication as a separately authorized step.

## BLOCKERS / MANDATE BOUNDARY

- Blocked for: AI production (tool unapproved), self-publication (no authorization), stock purchase (no budget/license plan), font suite installation (licensing act out of scope).
- Owner decisions actually required (named, so the ticket can be answered precisely): approve or reject the AI tool for production with a rights review; approve a stock budget with license evidence rules; grant a publication authorization when assets pass acceptance.
