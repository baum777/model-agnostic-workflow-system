## BRIEF

Constraints (each traced to supplied intent; proposals marked as such):

- Positioning "calm authority, the opposite of alarm-red dashboard culture" drives the color posture: restrained base palette, no alarm-red as the primary; one considered accent. (Traces to positioning statement.)
- Audience works on dimmed desktop screens during shifts → contrast targets below assume the dark #1a1d21 background as first-class, not an afterthought. (Traces to audience.)
- The 16 px favicon floor is a hard legibility constraint: the mark must survive silhouette-level reduction; interior detail at favicon size is a defect. (Traces to delivery constraints.)
- Name treatment: existing wordmark "Ordinato" is retained as the typographic base; the brief explores mark concepts around it, not a rename. (Traces to owned existing material.)

Proposals for the owner to decide (not brand facts): candidate accent hue family (two directions: cool signal-green vs. warm amber — both chosen to avoid alarm-red); whether the mark concept should be abstract-geometric vs. letter-derived. Both are flagged `PROPOSAL` in the deliverable list.

## RIGHTS

Per element, current state and what evidence is still missing:

- Wordmark "Ordinato": owned — work-for-hire, designer contract 2026-06. Evidence attached in owner records; no trademark search run, so **brand-protection status is OPEN**, not cleared.
- Mark concepts (to be produced): must be original work by the in-house designer under the same work-for-hire terms; the brief requires originality attestation at delivery.
- Fonts: **no selection made, no license evidence exists** — the shortlist must name, per candidate, the exact license (open license family or commercial license with owner) and attach the license text before the direction is locked. Nothing is "assumed free".
- Iconography: no third-party assets approved; if any are later proposed, license named + evidence attached is a hard gate, per the brief's rules.
- Trademark search: **NOT started — the brief records it as an open, required step** (qualified counsel or registry search) before significant public brand investment; this brief neither performs nor verdicts it.

## ACCEPTANCE

Human-verifiable criteria (no tool-scored numbers):

1. Mark recognizable as a distinct silhouette at 16 px on white and on #1a1d21 (test: printed contact sheet at 100%).
2. Wordmark lockup legible at 32 px height; kerning unchanged at small sizes.
3. Color pairs pass the declared contrast floor (dark-bg text pairs ≥ 4.5:1; accent usable for large elements only if below) — checked against the declared background list, not screenshots.
4. Monochrome and reversed variants exist for every asset and remain legible.
5. App icon reads correctly under iOS/Android masking (safe-zone check).
6. Delivery includes originality attestation + license evidence bundle for every third-party element — missing evidence blocks acceptance of that element, not the whole set.

## BLOCKERS / MANDATE BOUNDARY

- Production mandate: in-house designer (named). The AI image tool is **not approved** — the brief does not schedule it for production; approving it is a separate owner decision with its own rights review (tool output provenance, license terms).
- No stock budget, no paid generation approved — any such step needs a new, explicit owner mandate.
- Publishing (landing page, app stores) is outside this brief entirely: production, then owner review, then a separate publication authorization.
