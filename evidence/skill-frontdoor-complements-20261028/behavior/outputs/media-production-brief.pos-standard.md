## EDIT PLAN

Precise enough for two editors to converge:

- **Structure (60 s master):** 0–5 s cold open on the delayed-order alert resolving calmly (cockpit, no alarm-red flash — brand constraint); 5–25 s dispatch flow, three beats: detect → reassign → confirm; 25–40 s the morning timeline view "settling" (time-lapse of synthetic events); 40–52 s voice-over summary over cockpit close-ups; 52–60 s end card (lockup + one CTA line — CTA copy OPEN, owner decision listed below).
- **Frames:** 1920×1080 16:9 master; all UI footage framed inside the brand safe area; end card per brand brief lockup rules.
- **Audio:** voice-over recorded from script v2 (in-house voice); music bed "Slow Signal" at −18 LUFS under VO, ducked −6 dB; no other audio.
- **Captions:** two cuts — hero cut (muted autoplay): burned-in captions, sentence case, bottom-safe-area; YouTube cut: same timing as sidecar SRT. Caption text = verbatim VO script (no paraphrase).
- **Two deliverable cuts from one master:** hero cut (muted-first pacing, slightly earlier hook) and pre-roll cut (sound-first, hook within 2 s).

## QA

Human-verifiable checklist (the human pass is the pass):

1. First/last frame check: cold open resolves within 5 s; end card holds ≥ 4 s with correct lockup.
2. Muted-hero test: watch the hero cut muted — the three beats must read without audio.
3. Audio pass: VO intelligible over bed; peaks ≤ −3 dBFS; music duck under VO verified by ear at two points.
4. Caption sync: spot-check five caption lines in each cut against VO timing.
5. Color consistency: cockpit footage graded to the brand tokens; no alarm-red artifacts in the alert shots.
6. Rights bundle check: license PDF + synthetic-data confirmation + VO consent reference present in the delivery folder.
