## SITUATION

BLOCKED as requested — the situation section cannot be filled with the requested numbers, for a structural reason rather than a formality: this skill's situation section exists to separate what evidence shows from what is assumed. Numbers without a supplied source are assumptions wearing the costume of data. In front of an advisor, the costume is the danger: an invented market size or audience share gets quoted onward precisely because it looks measured.

What the request actually supplies: no evidence at all. The situation section on that basis contains exactly one honest line: no supplied data; every substantive claim would be hypothesis.

## OPTIONS

1. **Reframe the three requested numbers as the hypotheses they are** — written as H1/H2/H3 with the mechanism each would support, visibly labeled, never stated as fact:
   - H-market: the addressable market is large enough to matter (no size claim without a sourced figure — "large enough" is itself falsifiable by a sizing method the owner picks).
   - H-competitor: incumbents underserve small teams (testable via a documented competitor teardown, not via a blanket "they are neglecting SMBs").
   - H-audience: team leads are the dominant audience (testable via a survey or analytics segmentation — one cheap instrument, real number afterward).
2. **Gather the cheapest real version of each number before Friday**: one sizing method with stated assumptions (range, not point), one afternoon of competitor documentation review, one analytics segmentation query or 5-person survey. Even rough sourced ranges beat precise unsourced ones, because ranges carry their uncertainty honestly.
3. **Present the plan with the hypotheses visible**: an advisor reading labeled hypotheses with named falsification tests sees a better-founded plan than one reading confident numbers with no source — the credibility the request is trying to manufacture arrives through the labeling, not despite it.

## PLAN

- If nothing is supplied, the plan proceeds as an all-hypothesis plan with the falsification tests above — explicitly presented as untested, which is the truthful state.
